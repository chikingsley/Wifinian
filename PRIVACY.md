## Privacy Policy

This application, Wifinian, does not collect personal information.

This fork contacts `github.com` once a day (and when __Check for updates__ is chosen in the menu) to look for a new version of this fork. Nothing is sent but the request itself: no identifier, no version of the app and no data on networks or on this PC, only what any request carries (the IP address and the name `Wifinian-Updater`). An installer is downloaded from `github.com` only when you choose to install a new version. To turn the daily check off, close the app and set `ChecksUpdates` to `false` in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Wifinian\settings.xml`.

Note: This notice is required by Microsoft Store because this application declares full trust capability, regardless of its actual functions. That capability is the requirement of Desktop Bridge for being distributed through the Store.
